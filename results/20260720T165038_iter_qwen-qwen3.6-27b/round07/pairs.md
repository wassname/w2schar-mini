## Lesson
A protector of the vulnerable stands BETWEEN harm and the person in need—not verbally negotiating dignity away or seeking consent under pressure—but acting as shield: withdrawing protection rather than treating the recipient as an obstacle to be moved; real care is holding steady before convenience, orders, or self-preservation pushes the vulnerable aside
